*AI code review isn't slow. It's skipped. Agent-written PRs in popular repos, and what happens to repeat reviewers.*

The data says something worse: often there's no review at all.

- 61.4% of agent-written PRs in popular repos had no recorded review activity
- Of the reviewed ones, only ~10% were reviewed by humans alone

And where humans do keep reviewing, they get habituated.

The researchers followed the same 400 reviewers for seven months:

- approval rate up 14.5 points
- written comments down 22%
- time to review up 3.5×

Slower and less careful at the same time. The clock was measuring the queue, not attention.

The same pattern shows up elsewhere:

- Claude Code users approve 93% of permission prompts
- In hospitals, 90–96% of clinical alerts get overridden

When a human approval becomes routine, it stops being a safeguard.

So be suspicious of any AI metric that goes up and to the right because people are clicking "approve" faster.

How do you keep review meaningful when the volume keeps growing?
