*Ask, don't delegate. Comprehension test taken without AI, after learning an unfamiliar library.*

In a randomised study, 52 developers worked with an unfamiliar library. Then everyone took a comprehension test without AI:

- Hand-coding group: 67%
- AI group: 50%

The split inside the AI group is what matters:

Developers who used AI to ask conceptual questions kept their understanding.

Developers who used it to do the task lost about 17 points.

Other fields show the same pattern:

- Clinicians exposed to AI assistance detected less in later procedures done without it (28.4% → 22.4%)
- In a support centre, novices gained 34% with AI while experienced staff gained almost nothing

The uncomfortable loop: verification is the bottleneck of AI-assisted work, and verification needs the very skill that delegation wears down.

My rule now: when I'm in unfamiliar territory, I ask it to explain, not to write.

Have you noticed any skill getting rusty since you started using AI daily?

## Sources
