*Unchecked AI output costs you trust. How colleagues react to low-effort AI work they receive.*

A cross-industry study on low-effort AI work passed between colleagues found:

- 42% saw the sender as less trustworthy
- About a third were less willing to work with them again
- After one bad AI submission, receivers started distrusting everything else that person sent

And on the receiving end:

- 77% said AI-generated work takes longer to review, averaging nearly two hours of rework each time

There's a structural reason. When I hand you my AI output, I hand you a harder problem than the one I had. I at least framed the task. You're reviewing work with nobody's intent behind it.

As one developer put it in another study:

"It's not your code you're reviewing, it's somebody else's."

Generating is cheap now. Your reputation for sending things that are actually checked is not.

Have you received "AI slop" from a colleague? How did it change how you read their next message?
