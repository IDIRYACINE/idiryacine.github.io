*One core, one feature. Four filters every product idea had to pass.*

A core value is one feature. Only one.

One feature that, on its own, would justify someone paying.

Not a bundle. Not a list. Not a platform.

If describing it needs the word "and", it's two products.

Every idea had to pass four filters before it got any more time:

1. Do WE have this problem?

If it's not a pain inside our own team, it's a hard no. No report, no market sizing, just: would we use it tomorrow?

2. One feature, one core.

The "and" test.

3. Does it recur?

Something used again and again, not a one-off.

4. The 30-second test.

Write it as a landing page. If it doesn't sell in 30–60 seconds, it isn't precise enough yet: sharpen it or drop it.

Most of my ideas died at #1 or #4.

The ones that survived were simpler than anything I'd started with.

What's your filter before you build something?
