*More agents, more collisions. Conflict rate between concurrent agent-written PRs.*

Across 33,000+ agent-written PRs:

- 79% were open at the same time as another agent's PR
- Pairs from the same agent conflicted 19.8% of the time
- Pairs from different agents conflicted 41.7% of the time

Mixing tools roughly doubled the collision rate.

And those are lower bounds. Only text conflicts were counted, not build breaks or semantic clashes.

The deeper problem: however many agents you run, everything lands in the same human review queue.

The queue is the constraint, and it doesn't scale with the agents.

More agents running doesn't automatically mean more finished work. Sometimes it just means more collisions waiting for one tired reviewer.

To be fair, the cross-agent sample is small (115 pairs) and comes from open source only. It's enough to question "multi-agent by default", not enough to rule it out.

Are you running several agents in parallel? What breaks first?
