*Better individually, more alike collectively. 300 writers, 600 independent judges.*

In a controlled experiment with 300 writers and 600 independent judges:

- AI-assisted stories scored higher individually
- They also converged about 10.7% more with each other

Individual quality went up while collective variety went down. The two moved in opposite directions.

It isn't a one-off. A meta-analysis of 19 studies across creative work found the same small but significant homogenising effect.

For a team, it's easy to miss:

Everyone is prompting the same model, so everyone gets nudged toward the same "good" answer.

Brand voice drifts toward average, and nobody notices until it's published.

And audiences can tell. In one poll, 78% said AI-made ads feel less authentic.

One finding gives me hope: giving the AI deliberately different personas measurably reduced the convergence in group brainstorming.

So use AI to find your own angle, not the average one.

Have you noticed your team's output starting to sound the same?
