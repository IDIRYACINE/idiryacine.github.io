*Feeling faster vs being faster. Experienced developers on their own repos, with AI (METR RCT).*

Afterwards, they said it had made them 20% faster.

Measured: they were 19% slower.

This is the METR trial: 16 experienced open-source developers, 246 real tasks on their own repositories, randomised per task.

Before anyone reposts this as "AI makes developers slower": it doesn't show that, and METR explicitly asks people not to read it that way. The slowdown was concentrated where AI has the hardest job: experts, in codebases they know deeply, with high quality bars.

The lesson I took is different:

How fast you feel is not evidence of how fast you are.

The perception gap kept people using the tool even while it cost them time, and from the inside the cost was invisible.

So:

- Measure, don't self-report
- Compare the same person over time
- Count accepted work, not produced work

Have you ever actually timed yourself with and without AI?
